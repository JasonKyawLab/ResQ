## Full description (3074/4000)

ResQ sends your location and a short message to nearby phones that also have ResQ, using Bluetooth. It needs no mobile signal, no Wi-Fi, no internet and no account.

IMPORTANT, PLEASE READ FIRST
• ResQ does not contact emergency services and cannot promise that anyone will receive your signal. If you can, call your local emergency number first.
• It only works if another phone with ResQ is within Bluetooth range, or can be reached through other ResQ phones passing the signal on.
• The broadcast is not encrypted. Anyone close enough, with ResQ or a Bluetooth scanner, can read your location and message. Do not put sensitive details in your message.

HOW IT WORKS
If you need help: press Start. Your phone broadcasts your position and message over Bluetooth Low Energy. The first signal goes out at once, even before GPS has a position, and a fresh position follows as soon as there is one. Pick a ready-made message such as "Trapped" or "Need medical help", or write your own. Add a line saying where you are, such as your room or floor, and it goes out with every SOS.

If you want to help: switch on Scan. Your phone listens for SOS signals nearby and lists each one with its message, position, how long ago it was heard and how strong the signal is. Copy the coordinates to open them in a map app. While scanning, your phone also passes the signals it hears on to other phones, so an SOS can travel further than one phone's range. Each card shows how many phones it was passed through.

When you press Stop, nearby phones are told you have ended the SOS and show it as marked safe, unverified. If a phone goes silent, others show "Signal lost" with the last known position and how old it is.

FEATURES
• One big Start button, ready-made messages, and a saved "My location" line
• Works with the screen off, as a foreground service with a notification
• Sends your position every 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or hourly with Battery saver
• Keeps broadcasting even if GPS is slow or missing, marked "position unknown"
• Comes back after the phone closes the app, if the SOS was running
• Live list of nearby SOS with position age and signal strength
• Dismiss entries or clear them all, and a guide that explains every screen
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no account

GET READY BEFORE AN EMERGENCY
Open the guide in Settings. Save your "My location" line, allow Bluetooth, location and notifications, and turn off battery optimisation for ResQ so that Android does not stop it.

PERMISSIONS
• Bluetooth: to broadcast, to listen, and to read a full message from a nearby phone.
• Location: to put your position in your SOS, and because Android requires it to scan for Bluetooth. ResQ does not ask for background location.
• Notifications: to show that ResQ is broadcasting or scanning.

PRIVACY
ResQ has no internet permission and no server. Nothing is sent to the developer. Your location and message go only to nearby phones over Bluetooth, as described above. Received signals are kept on your phone for up to 72 hours. Privacy policy: https://resq-privacy.pages.dev
